About Sri Veerabhadra Nursery & Gardens

A family-run nursery established in 2005, spanning 15 acres in Kadiam, Andhra Pradesh. We grow 500+ healthy plant varieties and supply farms, layouts, resorts, and landscapes across India. Every healthy plant contributes to a greener future.
