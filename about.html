Our Story

A family legacy rooted in agriculture

Our journey reflects steady growth, practical knowledge, and a commitment to supplying quality plants that perform well in the field.

  • Family involved in agriculture for generations

    Our background in farming and cultivation shaped the nursery’s practical, hands-on approach.

  • Started as a fruit garden

    The business began as a fruit garden, building deep familiarity with plant care and growth cycles.

  • Converted into a nursery in 1995

    We formalized the nursery in 1995 and expanded our capabilities to meet wider demand.

  • Supply plants across India

    Today we serve customers nationwide with healthy, dependable nursery-grown plants.
